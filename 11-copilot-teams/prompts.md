# 11 — Copilot in Teams: Prompts

Use these prompts to plan, run and follow up on the launch meeting for your AI Usage Guide.

> **Session guide:** Part 1 runs in Copilot Chat. Parts 2 and 3 run in the Copilot pane inside a Teams meeting, so start a meeting first (pair up if you are in a training room). Parts 4 and 5 run in a Teams chat or channel.

---

## Part 1 — Draft the meeting agenda

**Where:** Copilot Chat, Work IQ on

```
I am running a 30-minute Teams meeting to launch our new AI
Usage Guide to my department. Draft an agenda with 4 items and
a time for each. Include a short presentation of the guide,
time for questions, and agreeing next steps. Keep each item to
one line.
```

Copy the agenda into the Loop agenda in your meeting invitation (turn on **Teams meeting**, then select **Add an agenda** at the bottom of the form), then turn on **Facilitator** with the **Turn on** link next to the Teams meeting toggle, or under **Options > Copilot and other AI**.

---

## Part 2 — During the meeting

**Where:** Copilot pane in the meeting (select **Copilot** in the meeting controls)

Ask these as the meeting goes on. Each one takes a few seconds.

```
Summarise the meeting so far in 5 bullet points.
```

```
What questions have people asked about the AI Usage Guide,
and which ones have not been answered yet?
```

```
Where do people disagree, and what are the different views?
```

```
List the decisions we have made so far.
```

```
Suggest action items from this meeting, with an owner for each
where it is clear who agreed to do it.
```

> **Try it:** ask the "questions not answered yet" prompt about five minutes before the end, then use the answer to close the open questions while everyone is still in the meeting.

---

## Part 3 — After the meeting

**Where:** the meeting chat, **Recap** tab. If Copilot ran during and after the meeting, you can keep asking it questions there.

```
Write a short follow-up message for the team channel. Thank
everyone for attending, list the 3 key decisions, and list the
action items with owners and due dates. Plain language, under
150 words.
```

```
A colleague missed this meeting. Summarise what they need to
know about the AI Usage Guide in under 100 words, including
anything they are expected to do.
```

```
Based on the questions people asked, which sections of the AI
Usage Guide are unclear and should be improved?
```

> **Note:** check the owners on every action item before you post the follow-up. Copilot can attach an action to the wrong person if two people spoke at once.

---

## Part 4 — Post the follow-up

**Where:** your team channel

Paste the follow-up message from Part 3 into the channel. If you want to adjust it first, use the Copilot option in the compose box if you have one, or paste it back into the Recap Copilot pane with:

```
Make this friendlier and add a line asking people to reply
in this thread with any further questions.
```

---

## Part 5 — Catch up on a busy chat

**Where:** any Teams chat or channel with a long conversation. Select **Copilot** at the top right.

```
Summarise the main points from the last 7 days.
```

```
What has been asked of me in this chat, and has anyone
followed up on it?
```

```
List any decisions made in this conversation, with the date
each one was made.
```
