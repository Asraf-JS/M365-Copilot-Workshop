**Asraf Jaafar Sidik** is a Microsoft Certified Trainer based in Malaysia, delivering hands-on corporate workshops on Microsoft 365, the Power Platform, data analytics and AI tools for organisations in banking, pharmaceuticals and logistics.

Asraf's courses are built around practice rather than theory. Most of each session is spent doing the work, using realistic business scenarios and step-by-step labs that participants can repeat back at their desks.

Areas of focus include Microsoft 365 Copilot, Excel, PowerPoint, Word, Teams and SharePoint, Power BI, Power Apps, Power Automate, and generative AI tools such as ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Google Gemini and NotebookLM.

This book is part of a series of participant guides that accompany Asraf's courses. Every guide in the series follows the same layout, so once you know one, you know them all.
